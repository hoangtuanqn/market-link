1. What we hold

Everything below is something the product stores today. Nothing is collected for advertising, and nothing is sold on.

Your account
Full name, email, phone number and address. Your password is never stored as you typed it, only as a hash.
A Farmer's stall
Stall name, contact person, description, the markets and days you sell, your pickup windows and the map position of your stall.
Your orders
What you reserved and how much, the stall and market, your pickup date and time, the note you left for the stall, and the totals. Product names and prices are copied onto the order so it still reads correctly if the stall changes them later.
Order history
Every status change on an order, with the time and who made it.
Reviews
Your rating, your text, which order it came from, and any reply the stall wrote.
Favorites
The stalls, products and markets you saved.
Messages
What you and a stall write to each other in Messages, and when each message was read.
Notifications
The messages sent to you about your orders and restocks, and whether you have read them.
Assistant chats
Your messages, the assistant's replies, and the intent the assistant matched. See section 5.
Feedback
The type you chose and what you wrote. Feedback can be sent without an account, and then it carries no account with it.

MarketLink does not ask for or store card details, bank details or any payment information, because payment happens at the stall and never through the product.

2. Why we hold it

Your name and phone number exist so the stall knows who is collecting and can reach you on market morning. Your address exists to show how far a market is and to pre-fill directions. Your email is how you sign in and how you are told about an order.

Orders and their history exist so both sides can see what was agreed and when it changed. Reviews exist so other shoppers can judge a stall. Favorites exist so you can be told when something you wanted is back in stock.

3. Who can see it

The stall you order from
Sees your order and enough contact detail to hand it over on market day, and the messages you send it. It does not see your orders with other stalls.
Other shoppers
See your reviews and the name on them. They do not see your orders, your contact details or your favorites.
An admin
Sees accounts and Farmer applications in order to approve, suspend or deactivate them, and sees listings and reviews in order to moderate them. Admin reports are counts and totals, not lists of who bought what.
Anyone else
Nothing. Orders, contact details, favorites and notifications are never shown on a public page.

Every screen that takes an id checks on the server that the record belongs to you before showing it, so changing a number in the address bar does not open someone else's order. Which customer fields a stall sees is not defined

4. Cookies and sessions

When you sign in, MarketLink issues a short-lived token that your browser sends with each request, and a longer-lived token kept in a cookie so you are not signed out every few minutes. Your signed-in session is also cached on the server to keep sign-in fast. Signing out ends both.

Your cart is kept in your own browser until you place the order. Nothing in your cart reaches the server before you check out.

MarketLink sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Cookie banner: needed or not

5. The shopping assistant

The assistant answers questions about markets, stalls, pickup windows and products. Both sides of the conversation are stored, together with the intent the assistant matched to your question, so the team can see why it answered the way it did.

A guest can use it too. To keep a conversation together, your browser holds a random session key that is not tied to any account; if you are signed in, the conversation is attached to your account instead.

Your words are never turned into a database query. The assistant matches your question to one of a fixed set of prepared lookups, so what you type is only ever a search term. Ask it about produce and pickup times, not about anything private.

6. What these pages load from elsewhere

OpenStreetMap
Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap servers, which see your IP address and roughly which part of the map you are looking at. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors. The directions link opens OpenStreetMap in a new tab.
Google Fonts
The two typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when the page opens.

MarketLink does not use Google Maps, so there is no maps key and no account behind the map.

7. Your choices

You can see and change your name, phone, email and address from your account page at any time. You can remove anything you saved to favorites, and mark notifications as read.

Some things stay even after you change them: an order keeps the product names and prices it was placed with, and its status history is not rewritten, because both sides rely on that record. A review you wrote stays until you or an admin takes it down.

Account deletion, data export and retention are not defined
