About MarketLink

MarketLink connects Farmers at local farmers markets in Ho Chi Minh City with the people who shop there. Farmers publish what they will bring this week; customers reserve it and pick it up at the stall.

Why it exists

Market stock is usually announced on a chalkboard or by word of mouth. People arrive to find the good things gone, or make the trip to a stall that is closed for the week. Farmers, in turn, have no easy way to publish their weekly inventory, take pre-orders, or keep in touch with regulars. MarketLink puts the week's stock, the stall's location and a pre-order queue in one place.

What it does not do

  • No online payment. You pay the Farmer at the stall when you pick up.
  • No delivery. Every order is picked up at the market, in the stall's pickup window.
  • No certification. MarketLink does not verify licences or organic claims; ask the Farmer.
  • One account can be shared within a family. The system does not tell people apart inside an account.
