MarketLink prototype

Clickable screens for the three roles, built on the Hang tag design system and the requirements in .ai/REQUIREMENTS.md. Everything the SRS or the decisions do not define is marked with a red TODO on the screen and listed at the bottom of this page.

How to openServe the repo root (for example python3 -m http.server) and open docs/prototype/index.html. Maps need a connection for OpenStreetMap tiles.
Demo accounts in the screensCustomer Khang · Farmer Cô Tư Garden (Nguyễn Thị Tư) · Admin. Public pages render as a guest; add ?as=customer to see them signed in.
Prototype barBottom left on every screen: role, FR ids, a list of all screens and the open questions on that screen.
What it is notStatic HTML with demo data from data.js. No API calls, no real sign-in. Real components are TSX in frontend/src.

Screens by role

Requirements coverage

Which screen carries each MUST or SHOULD requirement. Requirements that live in the backend or are cross-cutting have no screen of their own and are listed after the table.

Requirements without a dedicated screen

  • FR-005 RBAC and FR-006 sign out: server-side; the header menu changes per role and sign out sits in Account / the drawer.
  • FR-031 stock deduction, FR-038 status history, FR-039 auto-complete job: backend behaviour; the order detail screens show the history and the 24-hour note.
  • FR-043 real email: NICE, not designed. FR-080 responsive and FR-084 four states: applied on every screen; key lists show their loading, empty and error states inline.
  • FR-113 unread count and FR-117 header previews: the message bubble and the bell in the header of every signed-in screen (and the bell in the Farmer and Admin shells) open a preview of the latest three on hover, click or Enter; Esc or a click outside closes it.
  • FR-110 to FR-117 (chat) are proposals from the chat spec §4, built in the app but not yet in .ai/REQUIREMENTS.md. The order part of FR-114 waits for the order module.
  • FR-100 to FR-102 seed data: data.js mirrors the shape (4 markets, 10 Farmers, orders in all 6 states, one account per role).

Open questions

Each item is something the SRS, the decisions or the API contract do not define. The prototype shows one reasonable rendering and marks it; the team decides. Regenerate this list with python3 docs/prototype/collect-todos.py.

    Design notes

    Followed from the design system

    Colors only through tokens; Patrick Hand for what the stall or market says (names, prices, greetings, the “From the stall” note) and Chivo for what the system says; one handwritten heading per screen; paper tag cards with the 2 px edge; the punched hole only on product tags; the tilted yellow price tag; dashboards on the lighter surface-quiet; a written reason next to every locked button; “pay at the stall” wherever a total appears; Leaflet + OSM pins with the square/round distinction.

    Choices made for these screens

    Home opens with the week's dates and a green board of which market is open when, instead of a hero photo. Order detail uses a dotted step strip plus a history timeline for FR-038. The Farmer's weekly template is a product × day grid with greyed non-selling days. Admin gets a seventh menu item, Settings, for categories, announcements and the feedback inbox because the SRS lists them but the reference menu had no home for them. Reports use plain proportional bars, no chart library. Stall names carry their descriptive half in English (Cô Tư Garden, Củ Chi Goat Farm); people's names and Ho Chi Minh City place names stay as they are, which is a deviation from the brand book's Voice section for FE1 to confirm.
