# StallCard

A Farmer's stall card, used in the list of Farmers at a market and in the map popup (FR-010, FR-011).

- The consumer passes `stallName`, `contactPerson`, `markets`, `days`, `pickup`, `rating`, `reviews`, `distance`, `onView`, `onDirections`.
- Replace the initial-letter circle with a real stall photo when one exists. Never render this card on public pages for a `suspended` Farmer (D-09).
