# Logo

The brand mark: a hang tag with a real punched hole and a piece of twine threaded through it, next to "MarketLink" hand-lettered in Patrick Hand.

- The consumer passes `size` (px, default 32), `href` (when it links to home), and `markOnly` (mark without the wordmark, for favicons and tight spots).
- The mark is drawn in `currentColor`, and the hole is cut out with `fill-rule: evenodd` so the background shows through. To recolor it, set `color` on the parent: `on-board` on `board`, `ink` or `brand` on paper.
- Standalone files live in the Logos asset group: `marketlink-mark.svg` (`brand` ink) and `marketlink-mark-light.svg` (`on-board` ink, for dark backgrounds).
- Don't round it, add a shadow, or put it inside a colored box. Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the mark's height around it.
